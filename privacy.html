隐私政策 — TaoMind（道心）Privacy Policy — TaoMind

最后更新日期：Last updated: 2026 年 8 月 28 日August 28, 2026

1. 概述1. Overview

TaoMind（道心）（以下简称"我们"）尊重并保护您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明当您使用 TaoMind iOS 应用（以下简称"本应用"）时，我们如何收集、使用、存储和披露您的信息。TaoMind ("we", "us") respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose your information when you use the TaoMind iOS app (the "App").

使用本应用即表示您同意本隐私政策中的条款。By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. 我们收集的信息2. Information We Collect

2.1 您主动提供的信息2.1 Information You Provide

  • 您提出的问题/描述：您在使用"求取智慧"或"每日修行"功能时输入的文本内容。Your questions and reflections: the text you enter when using Seek Wisdom or the daily practice feature.
  • 场景类型：您在提问时选择的场景标签（商业决策、领导力、职业发展、个人成长、人际冲突等）。Scenario tags: the context you select when asking (business decisions, leadership, career, personal growth, relationships, etc.).
  • 语言偏好：您选择的界面语言（中文或英文）。Language preference: the interface language you choose (Chinese or English).
  • 通过 Apple 登录：当您使用"通过 Apple 登录"时，我们会接收 Apple 生成的唯一标识符，以及您选择与 App 共享的电子邮箱（可以是 Apple 隐藏邮箱）和姓名，用于在您的设备间同步修行记录。我们不会获取您的 Apple ID 密码。Sign in with Apple: when you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique identifier generated by Apple, plus the email (which may be a private relay address) and name you choose to share with the App — used to sync your practice across your devices. We never receive your Apple ID password.

2.2 自动收集的信息2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • 使用数据：我们收集基本的交互数据，如每日经文获取次数、提问与打卡时间戳、打卡天数等，用于提供连击统计、珍藏卡发放并改善服务质量。Usage data: basic interaction data such as verse fetches, check-in timestamps, and streak counts — used to provide streak statistics, run the card collection, and improve the service.
  • 匿名功能统计：我们收集应用内关键功能的使用统计（例如：付费页面展示次数、购买开始/成功、每日打卡与纪念卡解锁、日志保存等事件计数）。这些统计仅包含事件名称与功能参数（如触发场景、订阅套餐编号、卡片编号），不包含您输入的任何文字内容，且在未登录时完全匿名。仅用于了解哪些功能对用户有帮助，以改进产品。Anonymous feature analytics: we collect usage counts for key in-app features (e.g., paywall views, purchase starts/completions, daily check-ins and card unlocks, journal saves). These statistics contain only event names and feature parameters (such as the scenario triggered, subscription plan ID, or card number) and never include any text you enter; when signed out they are fully anonymous. They are used solely to understand which features help users and to improve the product.
  • 设备信息：应用版本号、操作系统版本（用于兼容性分析）。Device information: app version and OS version, used for compatibility analysis.

2.3 我们不收集的信息2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • 除您通过"通过 Apple 登录"主动共享的信息外，我们不收集您的姓名、电子邮件地址、电话号码等个人身份信息。Apart from what you choose to share via Sign in with Apple, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other direct identifiers.
  • 我们不收集精确位置数据。We do not collect precise location data.
  • 我们不收集通讯录、相册等设备敏感数据；麦克风仅在您主动使用语音输入功能时使用，录音内容仅用于转成文字，不做其他用途、不予保存。We do not access contacts, photo libraries, or other sensitive device data; the microphone is used only when you actively use voice input — the audio is transcribed to text and never stored or used for anything else.
  • 我们不会将您的数据出售给第三方。We never sell your data to third parties.

3. 信息的使用方式3. How We Use Information

我们收集的信息用于以下目的：We use the information we collect to:

  1. 提供核心服务：将您的问题发送至 AI 引擎以生成智慧解答。Provide core services: send your questions to the AI engine to generate guidance.
  2. 存储您的记录：将您的提问、感悟和获得的智慧内容保存在服务器上，以便您在"修行日志"功能中随时回顾，并在登录设备间同步。Store your records: save your questions, reflections, and received guidance on our servers so you can revisit them in the practice journal and sync them across signed-in devices.
  3. 提供打卡与收藏功能：依据打卡天数发放修行珍藏卡并保存收集进度。Power streaks & the card collection: grant collectible cards based on your check-ins and keep your collection progress.
  4. 改善产品体验：分析匿名使用趋势，优化回答质量。Improve the product: analyze anonymous usage trends to improve answer quality.
  5. 客户支持：在您联系我们时帮助排查问题。Customer support: help troubleshoot issues when you contact us.

4. 数据存储与安全4. Data Storage & Security

  • 存储位置：您的数据存储在我们的云服务器上（域名 taomindapp.com，托管于腾讯云）。Storage location: your data is stored on our cloud servers (domain taomindapp.com, hosted on Tencent Cloud).
  • 传输安全：所有数据传输均通过 HTTPS 加密。Transport security: all data in transit is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • 保留期限：我们会保留您的记录，直至您通过应用内功能删除，或通过联系我们要求删除。Retention: we keep your records until you delete them in the App or ask us to delete them.
  • 数据删除：您可以在"修行日志"中单条删除记录。如需完全删除所有数据，请通过下方联系方式联系我们。Deletion: you can delete individual entries in the practice journal. To erase all of your data, contact us using the details below.

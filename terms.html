2. 服务描述2. Description of Service

TaoMind 是一个基于 AI 的智慧指引应用，借鉴《道德经》和《金刚经》等经典哲学文本的智慧，为用户提供反思性的见解。主要功能包括：TaoMind is an AI-powered guidance app that draws on classical texts such as the Tao Te Ching and the Diamond Sutra to offer reflective insight. Its main features include:

  • 每日经文：每天推送一条经典哲学语录与反思问题。Daily Verse: a classical quote and a reflective question each day.
  • 求取智慧：用户描述情境，AI 生成包含经典引文、解读、反思和实践指引的结构化回答。Seek Wisdom: you describe a situation; the AI returns structured guidance with classical quotes, interpretation, reflection, and practice suggestions.
  • 每日修行与珍藏卡：记录每日感悟完成打卡，并依据打卡进度解锁道德经主题珍藏卡。Daily practice & collectible cards: record daily reflections to check in and unlock Tao Te Ching collectible cards as your streak grows.
  • 修行日志：保存和回顾历史智慧对话记录。Practice journal: your past sessions, saved and searchable.
